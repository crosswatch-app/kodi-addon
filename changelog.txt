CrossWatch for Kodi Changelog
=============================

v1.0.0~beta1 (2026-10-07)
-------------------------
New Features:
  - Reports Kodi playback to CrossWatch over its add-on webhook
    (/webhook/kodiwatcher, contract 1.4): start, pause, resume,
    progress, stop and a five-minute ping, with the viewers who
    watched.
  - Works out who is watching inside one Kodi profile: smart playlist
    membership of the show first, then the active Kodi profile, then a
    question at the end of playback that is remembered per show.
  - Pairing with an address and a one-time code from CrossWatch, Link
    from CrossWatch over JSON-RPC with a confirmation on the TV, and
    Unpair. Connection changes take effect without a Kodi restart.
  - Settings screens for viewers and their playlists or profiles, and
    for remembered answers (change, forget, forget all; shows a
    playlist covers are marked).
  - Completed watches are kept in outbox.json through an outage and a
    restart (7 days, 200 entries) and replayed once CrossWatch is back;
    every other event is sent only within two minutes.
  - PlexKodiConnect playback is skipped by default, and labelled when
    it is reported.
  - Supports https:// addresses for CrossWatch; a certificate the
    add-on does not trust is reported with its own message instead of
    a generic connection failure.
  - Pairing and Link failures are shown in an OK dialog instead of a
    toast notification, since Kodi's toast truncates longer messages.
  - Added icon.png as the add-on icon.

Internal:
  - The token travels only in the X-CrossWatch-Token header.
  - Gates: ruff, pyright with Kodistubs, vulture and pytest with a
    coverage floor, from a hash-pinned lock, in CI and pre-commit.
